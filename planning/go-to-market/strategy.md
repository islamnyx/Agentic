# Go-To-Market — Strategy

**Target buyer**: CTO, IT director, or cybersecurity manager at companies using multiple AI providers who need central governance.

**Core message**: "You don't need to replace Claude, Gemini, n8n, or your existing AI agents. Eclipse gives you one place to control what they can access, what they can do, and which actions require approval."

1. **Credibility first**: publish red-team demos and write-ups (agent attacked, policy blocks it, full audit trail shown). Security community reach is a real asset.
2. **Design partners**: 2-3 organizations using 2+ AI frameworks, heavily discounted or free pilot in exchange for feedback and a reference.
3. **Paid pilot to contract**: convert one pilot into a real contract and case study.
4. **Channel partners**: local integrators, consultancies, system vendors who already have the customer relationship.
5. **Expand** to nearby francophone and North African markets (similar language and regulation).

**Why not compete on agent building**: Google AI Studio and Claude Cowork already offer visual agent building within their platforms. Eclipse wins by being the independent control plane — not another place to build agents.
