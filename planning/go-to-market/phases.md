# Go-To-Market — Phases

| Phase | Goal | Success signal |
|-------|------|----------------|
| 0-3 months | Customer discovery: 15-20 interviews with CTOs/IT directors using multi-provider agents | Clear picture of who has the pain and budget for cross-provider governance |
| 3-9 months | Prototype plus 2-3 design partners using Gemini+Claude+n8n | Partners actively using Eclipse Control Center to govern their agents |
| 9-18 months | First paid contracts, one reference case | Revenue and a customer who'll vouch for Eclipse |
| 18+ months | Repeatable sales, second market | Pipeline not dependent on founders' networks |
