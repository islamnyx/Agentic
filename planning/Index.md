# Eclipse Agent OS — Company & Product Strategy

Eclipse Agent OS is the independent Agent Control Plane: one place to govern AI agents across every provider and framework. You don't need to replace Claude, Gemini, n8n, or your existing AI agents. Eclipse gives you one place to control what they can access, what they can do, and which actions require approval.
