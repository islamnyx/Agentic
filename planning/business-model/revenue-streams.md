# Business Model — Revenue Streams

Start with B2B SaaS subscriptions, with additional revenue from implementation and enterprise deployment.

| Revenue stream | Description |
|----------------|-------------|
| SaaS subscription | Monthly or annual platform access |
| Usage-based fees | Agent executions or other measurable resource consumption |
| Implementation | Integrations, configuration and onboarding |
| Enterprise | Private deployment, advanced governance and support |
| Developer ecosystem | Later: SDK ecosystem and marketplace revenue |

## Pricing

Do not finalize pricing until you have interviewed prospects and tested willingness to pay.

## Algeria

Plan for DZD contracts and invoicing from day one.
