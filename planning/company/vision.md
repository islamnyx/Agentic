# Company — Vision, Mission, Positioning

## Vision

Become the independent control plane that organizations use to govern AI agents across every provider and framework — not another place to build agents.

## Mission

Help organizations operate, control, and govern AI agents across their entire business, regardless of where those agents were built.

## Positioning

**Eclipse Agent OS** — The independent Agent Control Plane.

You don't need to replace Claude, Gemini, n8n, or your existing AI agents. Eclipse gives you one place to control what they can access, what they can do, and which actions require approval.

## Core Belief

Security is enforced outside the language model — never rely on an agent's prompt for access control. The control plane must intercept and authorize sensitive tool calls before they reach business systems, no matter which framework built the agent.

## The problem with competing on "build agents"

Google AI Studio, Claude Cowork, and n8n already let businesses build, connect, and approve agents within their own platforms. Eclipse does not win by being another visual builder or another agent runtime.

Eclipse wins by being the **independent operating and control layer** for agents built anywhere — Gemini, Claude, OpenAI, n8n, custom Python, MCP — governing how they interact with business systems.
