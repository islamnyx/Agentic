# Market — Target Market

## Initial target market

Medium-sized businesses in Algeria, particularly organizations with repetitive operations and business software that agents could interact with.

## Early industries

- Distribution and wholesale: sales, orders, inventory and customer support
- Manufacturing: operations, maintenance and internal reporting
- Financial and accounting services: document processing and internal workflows
- Customer service businesses: support agents and CRM automation
- Technology companies: internal developer and operational agents

## Focus principle

Do not target every industry at once. Find one segment with a costly, repetitive problem and use it to establish your first successful deployment.
